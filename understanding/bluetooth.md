# Bluetooth Fundamentals

Connects through electromagnetic waves (123mm wavelength).

Communicating 1's and 0's through different waves:
* 1s --> 121mm
* 0s --> 124mm

A million 1s and 0s every second in a "spanding sphere"


## Speeds by Bluetooth Version (Theoretical Max)
• Bluetooth 1.0: ~700 Kbps
• Bluetooth 2.0 / 2.1: ~3 Mbps
• Bluetooth 3.0 / 4.0 / 4.2: Up to 24 Mbps (though often capped much lower in practice)
• Bluetooth 5.0 and newer: Real-world file transfers generally hover around 1.5 to 2 Mbps due to protocol overhead and power-saving design.


# Conclusion:
Bluetooth is not good for transmiting files. Just for pairing/discovering in this case.
